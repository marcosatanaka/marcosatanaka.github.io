About MusicBox

MusicBox is an award-winning app that helps you catalog, organize, and rediscover your music library:

  • Tag albums and create smart collections to make them easier to find.
  • Give your favorites a star rating.
  • Save new music for later without cluttering your library.
  • Instantly access your collection with Home Screen widgets.

MusicBox collects relevant metadata of albums and songs and is deeply integrated into the system with features such as Interactive Widgets, Share Sheet, App Shortcuts, iCloud, and more. It supports a wide range of platforms, and you can also add music manually by filling in all the required fields.

Check out everything new in MusicBox for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS 26.

Core Features

MusicBox is a modern app that supports the latest system features: Interactive Widgets, App Shortcuts, Action Extension, Context Menu, iCloud sync, dark mode, and more. It is available for the iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV.

  • Multiple Services: MusicBox supports albums and songs from Apple Music, Spotify, Deezer, Discogs, Bandcamp, Songwhip, AllMusic, RYM, and Odesli. Users can also add music from other platforms by manually filling in all the required fields.
  • Easy to Add: Add music from the Share Sheet, via drag and drop, with Shazam, by searching, or by importing from a playlist.
  • Tags: Apply tags to categorize your music and make it easier to find.
  • Auto-Tagging: Automatically tag albums and songs based on rules you set.
  • Deep Search: Search your music by attributes such as title, artist, genre, record label, tags, type, and notes.
  • Smart Search: Automatically filter albums and songs based on a set of rules.
  • Focus Filter: Configure a Focus mode to limit the music shown in MusicBox.
  • App Shortcuts: Automate workflows using MusicBox’s actions and data.
  • Interactive Widgets: Add and customize interactive widgets to your Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Desktop.
  • Shazam: Easily save music playing around you for later.
  • iCloud: Your database is automatically synced to your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV.

Price and Availability

MusicBox is free to download on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV. You can enjoy all features and add up to five items at no charge. MusicBox Premium lets you add unlimited items and comes in three options:


Monthly: $1.99/mo

Annual: $14.99/yr

Lifetime: $29.99 (single purchase)


MusicBox requires at least iOS/iPadOS 17, macOS Sonoma, and tvOS 18.
