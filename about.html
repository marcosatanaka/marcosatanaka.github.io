About

I’m an indie app developer creating apps for Apple platforms and the founder of Loop Apps, where I focus on building products that feel intuitive, useful, and delightful.

I grew up in Maringá, Brazil, and studied Computer Science as well as System Analysis and Development. Over time, I specialized in developing apps for Apple platforms using technologies like Swift, UIKit, and SwiftUI.

Today, through Loop Apps, I work on several apps including MusicHarbor, MusicSmart, Play, and MusicBox. Outside of developing apps, I enjoy gardening, cooking, making coffee, running, staying active, listening to music, and reading. I’m motivated by the idea that thoughtfully designed technology can improve everyday life and make powerful tools accessible to more people.
