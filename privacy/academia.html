Academia - Exercícios Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Academia - Exercícios app (the "Application"), provided by Loop Apps.

Information Collection

The Application does not collect personal information or usage data. Loop Apps does not collect or have access to the exercises, load history, dates, descriptions, or notes you enter in the Application.

The Application does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect IP addresses, device identifiers, page views, time spent using the Application, or other usage statistics through the Application. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or marketing.

Local Storage and iCloud

The data you enter is stored locally on your device. When iCloud is available and enabled for the Application, your data is also synchronized with your private iCloud account so you can access it on your other devices.

iCloud is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. Your workout data is not sent to servers operated by Loop Apps.

Exporting and Importing Backups

You can choose to export your exercises and load history to a backup file and import that file on another device. Backup files are saved only to the location you select. Loop Apps does not receive these files. You control where they are stored and whether they are shared.

Accessing, Changing, and Deleting Your Data

You can view, edit, and delete your exercises and load history within the Application. When iCloud synchronization is enabled, these changes are synchronized with your private iCloud account and your other devices.

Deleting the Application removes its local data from that device, but does not necessarily remove data stored in iCloud or exported backup files. To delete synchronized records, delete them within the Application while iCloud synchronization is enabled. You can also manage the Application's iCloud storage through your device's iCloud settings. Delete exported backup files separately from the locations where you saved them.

External Services

If you choose to search for an exercise on YouTube, the Application opens a YouTube search using the exercise's name. Your use of YouTube and other external services is subject to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect personal information from users of any age, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes to the Application. The updated policy and its effective date will be posted on this page.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-10-08.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at marcosatanaka@gmail.com.
